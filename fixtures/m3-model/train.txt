The lantern rests beside the window. A reader opens the quiet book, follows a small map, and writes a careful note.
The reader closes the book, carries the map to the garden, and returns before the evening rain reaches the path.
Each morning the workshop records its tools. The blue box holds a ruler, two pencils, a brass key, and clean paper.
Mara checks the list, places each tool in its marked drawer, then writes the number 27 beside the repaired hinge.
The small robot rolls across the floor. It sees a red cup, pauses at the doorway, and waits for a clear signal.
When the signal arrives, the robot carries the cup to the table. Its wheels stop gently, and a green lamp confirms the task.
In the garden, three wooden signs point toward the well, the shed, and the old pear tree. A narrow path joins each place.
Rain taps the shed roof while the gardener counts six pots. Fresh soil fills every pot, and a seed rests near the center.
At noon, a neighbor brings bread, apples, and a jar of honey. The family shares lunch under a striped cloth beside the door.
The notebook keeps dates, names, and short observations. On page 14, a line says that warm wind dried the blue paint.
When clouds return, the workers cover the bench and carry their brushes inside. The last brush goes into a glass of water.
