# M7 source ledger

## Admitted for local research

The seed catalog lists six locally retained artifacts from M5. It pins the original byte count and SHA-256 of each ebook, the publisher, author, original catalog URL, acquisition date, rights evidence and split. The cache contains the original ebook files. M7 processes the text between the publisher's start and end markers. It preserves the UTF-8 bytes inside those boundaries, including line endings. Source-level processed hashes in the dataset manifest cover each successfully extracted UTF-8 document prefixed by its byte count as a little-endian u64. Per-shard SHA-256 covers the actual JSONL bytes. The archived notices preserve the source-specific credit and terms. [M7 catalog](sources-local-v1.json), [M5 source evidence](../m5-gpu/provenance/CPU_PREPARATION.md), [archived notices](../m5-gpu/provenance/pg-notices/).

The admitted editions contain original historical texts by authors who died between 1616 and 1898. Project Gutenberg's US status and terms concern the edition supplied by Project Gutenberg. For this local German run, the general German term for the historical text is 70 years after the author's death. Edition-specific rights can survive separately. Use the texts and manifests locally and preserve the credit and original notices. Recheck edition and territory before redistribution. Project Gutenberg's site directs automated corpus acquisition to mirrors. The six archived entries are marked local-cache only. [PG license and permission guidance](https://www.gutenberg.org/policy/license.html), [PG Terms of Use](https://www.gutenberg.org/policy/terms_of_use), [UrhG § 64](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/__64.html), [UrhG § 70](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/__70.html).

[Wikimedia Terms of Use](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Terms_of_Use) |
| Educational sources and general knowledge | Candidate for individually pinned material with affirmative CC BY or CC0 markings. An edition's title page and third-party material notices govern approval. Preserve title, author, license, URL and changes for CC BY redistribution. An institution-level open textbook label is insufficient for selecting an edition. [CC BY 4.0 legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.en) |
| Scientific material | Select individually licensed text from an approved repository channel. PMC's open access subset has mixed rights and retrieval restrictions. Its inclusion label alone does not grant the intended reuse. Validate article-level terms and retain DOI and version. [PMC Open Access Subset](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/tools/openftlist/) |
| Government texts | US federal works have a statutory rule with exceptions. Verify per-item authorship and third-party notices. German statutes have a separate statutory regime. Use primary-version identifiers to group revisions of one act. [17 USC § 105](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/t17/s105/), [UrhG § 5](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/__5.html) |
| Common Crawl derivatives, social-media exports, unclear license aggregations | Excluded. A collection-level license does not establish rights to the underlying records. |

New downloads require an explicitly approved pinned source list and byte budget. An opt-in catalog sets `download_url` for each authorized object. Review the original license before placing the object in the admitted catalog. The stored decision can be `local-research`, `redistribution-cleared` or `quarantined`. The engine enforces that decision and the presence of supporting fields. The license review itself is performed against primary evidence. The minimum intake record includes version, document identities and grouping, permissions, attribution, any territorial limits and the raw length/hash. Use the pinned source groups to preserve editions and known benchmark splits before deduplication and model work.
