## Additional operational hypothesis

M5’s faster C token/s did not yield higher raw-byte throughput (35.27k versus 35.69k bytes/s). Thus the practical efficiency ranking may depend on whether budget is counted as target positions, wall-clock, or bytes processed. Test quality-versus-measured-time curves, including evaluation/checkpoint overhead, rather than infer efficiency from token/s alone.

## Priority

Start with deterministic CPU tokenization/fragmentation analysis, then a paired five-seed 2k T replication using the exact frozen M5 pilot schedule and inputs. This estimates seed variability at the lowest neural-training cost and tests whether the single-seed BPB difference is reproducible. Next use a separately reported B exposure-matched comparison to probe H1; do not describe B as compute-matched. Only then spend on longer horizons or a backbone interaction.
